Hi! I’m Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests spanning LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, truthfulness and interpretability, and hallucination in vision-language models.

Academic background

- Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present)
- B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020–2024)

Research experience

- Research Intern, MINIMAX (Feb 2025–Present)
- Research Intern, Tencent WXG (Jun 2024–Sep 2024)
- Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab (Jun 2023–Dec 2023)
